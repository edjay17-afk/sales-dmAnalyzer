  • Categories. Every OR category is analysed. Hot Meals are category 39 Rice Meals and Fresh Food is categories 43 and 46 combined, as in the October reports. Other categories keep their OR names. BM slips carry no category, so each BM product takes the category it is sold under on the OR reports.
  • Checks. Each OR report is tied to its printed category totals and grand total, and each BM slip to its printed total.{% if A.base %} The baseline is the {{ A.base_days }} days of OR history before this period ({{ A.base_range[0] }} to {{ A.base_range[1] }}).{% endif %} Products are matched between OR and BM by item code. Expense items (free stickers and similar) are not counted.
  • Reading BM against sales. BM comes from earlier deliveries, so a product can show more BM than units sold in the same period. BM % on products selling under 5 a week swings widely, so treat those as flags.
  • Order suggestions. A small buffer above demand (Increase) is only suggested for short shelf life categories: Crunch Time, Rice Meals, Fresh Foods, Hot Foods, Bread and Bakery, Donuts, Dairy, Hotdog and City Cafe. Everything else is ordered at demand, trimmed or cut where there is real BM. There is no stock or delivery data, so count what is on hand before ordering.

How order numbers are calculated
  • Expected weekly demand is the average of this period's units sold (scaled to 7 days) and the product's baseline weekly rate over the prior 31 days. Products that started later are counted from their first sale; new products use this period only.
  • The waste rate is BM divided by (units sold + BM), blended with the baseline.
  • Increase: short shelf life category, waste under 3% and 5+ units/week; order demand + 10%. Hold: order demand. Trim: waste 10% to 25%; demand − 15%. Cut: waste 25%+ with BM in period; demand − 30%. Pause: zero sales; do not reorder until checked.
  • If a product sits on a fixed display minimum, lower the minimum rather than the order.
