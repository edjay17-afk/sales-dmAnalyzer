How order numbers are calculated
  • Expected weekly demand is the average of this period's units sold (scaled to 7 days) and the product's baseline weekly rate over the prior {{ A.cfg.baseline_days }} days. Products that started later are counted from their first sale; new products use this period only.
  • The waste rate is BM divided by (units sold + BM), blended with the baseline.
  • Increase: short shelf life category, waste under {{ (100 * A.cfg.increase_rate)|round(1) }}% and {{ A.cfg.increase_min_demand|round(1) }}+ units/week; order demand × {{ A.cfg.increase_factor }}. Hold: order demand. Trim: waste {{ (100 * A.cfg.trim_rate)|round(1) }}%+ (factor {{ A.cfg.trim_factor }}). Cut: waste {{ (100 * A.cfg.cut_rate)|round(1) }}%+ with BM in period (factor {{ A.cfg.cut_factor }}). Pause: zero sales; do not reorder until checked.
  • Entering an On hand count subtracts it from Raw 7d; Pack rounds net orders up to the nearest pack multiple.
