Branch reporting guide

How stores work

Each OR Sales Monitor and Bad Merchandise PDF carries a store header. When multiple stores are on file, a Store selector appears on the Upload, Report, Trends, and History pages so each branch's sales, shrinkage, and 31-day baseline stay separate.

Setting a Display Alias keeps reports readable while matching incoming PDFs by their exact printed store name.
